<objective>
Turn the landed Phase 5 installer into the safe filesystem boundary the ingest flow can reuse.

Purpose: Preserve the installer’s cache validation, exact markers, rollback, symlink defense, and byte-safe replacement while adding the one missing operation: replace the pre-marker source with an imperative master and refuse a stale final write.
Output: An explicit-target install transaction, a shared exact-region scanner, deterministic master preparation, and guarded atomic promotion.
</objective>

<interfaces>
Current installer contracts to preserve:
- `installBegin` / `installEnd` are the single exact physical-line marker constants.
- `resolveInstallPaths() (installPaths, error)` validates absolute HOME, ZDOTDIR, and ZSHPRO_HOME.
- `runInstallWithStoreInitialization(shell.Hooker, StoreInitializer) error` owns the loader + .zshrc transaction.
- `replaceManagedBlock(current, block []byte) ([]byte, error)` rejects malformed exact markers and preserves outside bytes.
- `prepareAtomicWrite(path, content []byte, defaultMode os.FileMode) (preparedWrite, error)` prepares a same-directory fsynced candidate.
- `(*preparedWrite).promote() error` renames the prepared candidate and syncs its directory.
</interfaces>
