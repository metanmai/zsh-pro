Purpose: Preserve the installer’s cache validation, exact markers, rollback, symlink defense, and byte-safe replacement while adding the one missing operation: replace the pre-marker source with an imperative master and refuse a stale final write.
Output: An explicit-target install transaction that can remain live through Commit, a durable adoption-mode contract, a shared exact-region/source-layout scanner, deterministic master preparation, guarded promotion outcomes, and the immutable Phase 6 execution-base record.
</objective>
