Output: Reviewed immutable execution base, neutral optional-revision outcomes, main-only Begin/Abort surface, pre-I/O reservation, hermetic private Git plumbing, cross-process-serialized recursive cleanup, aggregate initializer eligibility, and focused tests.
</objective>
